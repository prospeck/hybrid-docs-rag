# Northstar Engineering Handbook

## Working hours and on-call

Northstar uses flexible working hours with a four-hour collaboration window from 10:00 to 14:00 UTC. Engineers on a primary on-call rotation must acknowledge a page within five minutes. The secondary responder takes ownership if the primary has not acknowledged the page after five minutes.

## Code review policy

Every production change requires one approving review from a code owner. Changes that modify authentication, billing, encryption, or database schemas require two approvals, including one from the Security or Database Reliability team as appropriate. Authors may not approve their own pull requests.

## Deployment windows

Routine production deployments are permitted Monday through Thursday from 09:00 to 16:00 UTC. Friday deployments require an active SEV-1 remediation or written approval from the Director of Engineering. All deployments require a linked change ticket and a documented rollback plan.

## Learning and development

Each full-time employee receives an annual learning budget of USD 1,500. Conference travel requires manager approval before booking. Unused learning budget does not roll over to the next calendar year.

## Remote equipment

New employees may expense up to USD 800 for a desk, chair, monitor, and peripherals during their first 90 days. Laptops are issued separately and do not count against the equipment allowance.
